# Bondeth AI doodle illustrations

Additional creator, idea-capture, and developer scenes are documented with [their generation prompts and usage](illustrations-use-cases.md).

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. The selected set uses loose black fineliner contours, warm paper fills, simple expressive faces and saffron-orange scribbles. All five PNGs preserve transparency. `next/image` serves responsive optimized versions. Small waveform, headphone, sparkle, arrow and speech-bubble graphics remain native SVGs.

Assets are saved in `frontend/public/illustrations/`:

- `voice-studio-doodle-v2.png`: hero and speech-to-text scenes.
- `listener-doodle-v2.png`: text-to-speech and listening scenes.
- `voice-clone-doodle-v2.png`: voice-clone scenes.
- `notebook-doodle-v2.png`: notebook hints and transcript history.
- `access-doodle-v2.png`: account access and API-key empty states.

## Hero generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create a premium HAND-DRAWN DOODLE illustration for the homepage of a Khmer and English voice studio. It MUST be a whimsical minimalist ink doodle, NOT realistic, NOT painterly, NOT shaded portrait, NOT 3D, NOT polished vector geometry. Style: confident loose imperfect black fineliner lines, airy negative space, simple flat shapes, playful sophisticated editorial sketchbook. Think independent designer's playful ink doodle. Landscape 4:3 composition, genuinely transparent background. One friendly young Cambodian woman in bust/waist-up view, simple dot eyes and a tiny smiling mouth, slightly wavy solid dark hair, naturally speaking toward a microphone on a tiny tabletop. Very simple anatomically believable pose: both forearms rest on the tabletop, clear clean hands, no tangled arms, no legs or chair. Sweater has a few relaxed saffron-orange scribble strokes as the ONLY colored fill. Add one hand-drawn speech bubble with a simple waveform, a small floating page with three wavy ink lines, and two tiny ink asterisks, carefully balanced around the main figure. Black ink at a consistent 2-3px equivalent line weight, organic unsteady hand-drawn contours, a few little loose hatching strokes, the face extremely simplified, no realistic features or skin shading. Restrained saffron orange accent about 10 percent of image. No big enclosing arch, no background blob, no multicolor palette, no heavy geometric construction, no detailed hair strands, no giant cartoon eyes, no photo-realism, no watercolor, no texture, no gradients, no lettering or text, no logos. Professional delightful doodle that reads beautifully at 400px wide. Complete isolated artwork centered with comfortable outer margins.

## Companion generation prompts

Each used the hero PNG as a style reference, with this common prefix followed by the subject below:

Create a NEW companion HAND-DRAWN DOODLE for the same illustration set as the reference, which is a STYLE REFERENCE only. Match exactly its charming loose irregular black fineliner contours, black scribbled hair, very simple dot eyes and tiny smiles, warm off-white skin/paper fill, flat saffron-orange scribble accents, minimal little hatch marks. Genuine playful ink DOODLE, never realistic or painterly. Transparent background, landscape 4:3, centered, isolated illustration filling 85% of frame with clear margins. Black, warm off-white and saffron orange ONLY. Natural simple anatomy, no complicated full-body poses, no legs, no enclosing arch or blob backdrop, no text, no words, no logos. Clear readable at 300px wide.

One hand touches one headphone ear cup naturally. The other arm rests at the bottom of the composition. Two small floating ink music notes and a small waveform doodle convey listening. Keep the face very simple and small, same character proportions as reference.

### Voice clone

Two matching waist-up simple ink doodle characters of the same friendly Cambodian young woman, short black bob hair, one fully drawn wearing an orange scribbled sweater, the second drawn in dashed black ink outline and a few sparse orange hatching lines, recognizably her voice copy. They face slightly toward each other. In the gap a small hand-drawn waveform and a curved arrow connects them. No hands or arms crossing or touching. Simple balanced twin portraits, minimal clear composition.

### Notebook

A few loose black wavy lines on the paper suggest writing without any actual text. Natural perspective, light imperfect pen contours, loose hatching for a little depth. No people or faces.

### Access

A charming simple hand-drawn doodle still life: one friendly rounded padlock with an orange scribbled body and black ink shackle, a brass-style key rendered in black ink with small orange scribble accent leaning nearby, a small cream envelope behind. Two tiny ink asterisks, a few sketchy hatch strokes, loose unsteady contours. No people or faces. Keep simple, light and playful.
